Supporting Copy

2. The Problem
Headline
Great ideas don't always fail because they're bad ideas. Sometimes they run out of money before they get the chance to prove themselves.
For Founders
Accessing early-stage capital is difficult when you don't yet have the traction investors want to see.
For Investors
Early-stage African startup opportunities are difficult for individuals to discover, evaluate and access.
Closing Statement
We're building the bridge between the two.

3. How It Works
01 — Discover
Explore promising African startups raising early-stage capital.
02 — Invest
Invest an amount you're comfortable risking.
03 — Grow Together
As the company grows and its valuation increases, your investment has the potential to increase in value as well.
Illustrative Example
200 people × ₦100,000 = ₦20 million
Small individual investments can collectively provide a startup with meaningful early-stage capital.

4. Why Individual Investors?
Headline
You don't need millions to invest in Africa's future.
Core Message
We're creating a way for more people to participate in the growth of African startups without needing to be a traditional venture capitalist.
Key Ideas
Small tickets.
Invest an amount appropriate to your own risk appetite.
More investors.
A startup can raise capital from a broad community rather than relying on a single large investor.
Shared risk.
Individuals can potentially spread their investments across multiple early-stage businesses rather than concentrating their capital in one company.
Access to opportunities.
Discover early-stage African businesses that may otherwise be difficult for individual investors to access.
Risk Disclaimer
Early-stage startup investing is high risk. You can lose some or all of your investment.

FOOTER

Core Product Positioning
The landing page should not primarily position the product as:
"A crowdfunding platform for African startups."
Instead, position it as:
Making early-stage African startup investing accessible to more people.
Supporting Positioning Statement
We connect ambitious African founders with individuals willing to take an early bet on their future.
The deeper thesis is:
Founders need capital to become investment-ready.
Individuals want access to Africa's next generation of companies.
The platform connects the two.

Important Product Consideration
At this validation stage, avoid locking the messaging too tightly around the exact investment mechanism.
The core problem you're validating is:
Can we create a trusted way for individuals to participate in early-stage African startup opportunities while giving founders access to the capital they need to grow?
The eventual mechanism could involve equity crowdfunding or another regulated investment structure, depending on regulatory requirements and what your founder/investor research reveals.
